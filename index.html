Made for the people who run and use a campus mess

One place for students, mess staff, managers, and administrators

📅

Meal bookings

Reserve or cancel breakfast, lunch, snacks and dinner before configurable cutoffs so the kitchen has a clearer headcount.

🍽️

Daily and weekly menus

Publish recurring weekday menus, schedule date-specific changes and restore the weekly plan when an override is cleared.

✓

Attendance and QR workflow

Students can generate a short-lived QR pass for today's active booking. Staff can validate passes with a browser camera or use the booking-checked manual fallback.

★

Ratings and feedback

Collect student meal ratings and comments to make food-quality feedback part of the day-to-day workflow.

💬

Complaint tracking

Submit issues, attach an optional resized photo, and follow them through Pending, In Progress and Resolved states.

🥬

Inventory and low-stock alerts

Track ingredients, quantities and thresholds, with stock movements visible to mess managers.

🧾

Suppliers and purchasing

Maintain supplier details, register purchase orders and record received stock in the operations workspace.

📈

Demand estimates

Use recent bookings and observed check-in ratios to estimate meal demand, with a booking-only fallback when needed.

♻️

Waste and sustainability records

Log measured surplus or discarded food and review trends. Optional CO₂e estimates require a team-entered factor and source.

₹

Wallet ledger and fee records

Review administrator-recorded credits and fee history. Ledger credits can settle a demo fee but are not real or transferable funds.

🔔

Announcements and staff duties

Publish notices with read status and optional opt-in browser push; assign duties and update them individually or in bulk.

📊

Reports, search and administration

Explore role-scoped records, sort reports by multiple fields, export CSVs including purchase expenses, and manage access and activity.

MessMate follows an iterative project plan and keeps each role's records in one secure application.

About MessMate

MessMate is a campus dining project from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Chitkara University, Himachal Pradesh. It brings meal bookings, attendance, student feedback, inventory and mess operations into one role-based platform.

The project team is Aakash Rana (2311981005), Aditya Antwal (2311981035) and Anubhav (2311981098). The application includes separate student, staff, manager and administrator experiences for academic demonstration.

A practical academic prototype

The project uses role-based access across student, staff, manager and administrator dashboards. Attendance supports short-lived, booking-checked QR passes and manual fallback; notices support optional browser push; wallet fee payments use ledger credits; and real payment transfers require configured provider credentials.
